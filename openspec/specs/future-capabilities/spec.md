# Future Capabilities Specification

## Purpose

Document capabilities that are explicitly out of scope for the initial release and may be considered in later phases.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Animation

The system SHALL NOT provide animation capabilities in the initial release. Animation MAY be considered in a future release.

### Requirement: Analysis

The system SHALL NOT provide graph analysis capabilities (e.g. path finding, centrality) in the initial release. Analysis MAY be considered in a future release.
